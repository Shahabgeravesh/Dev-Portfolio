CourtReady Lite Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026
Product: CourtReady Lite (iOS / iPadOS)
Apple App ID: 6744664594

CourtReady Lite is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of the New York State Unified Court System or any government agency.

Summary

CourtReady Lite is designed to work offline. Study progress is stored on your device. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. No personal data is transmitted by the app.

No account required

You can use CourtReady Lite without creating an account, signing in, or providing a name, email address, or other profile information to the app.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising
  • No analytics
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No third-party network SDKs
  • No crash-reporting SDKs
  • No intentional transmission of study progress or personal data off the device by the app

Data stored on your device

CourtReady Lite may store the following information locally on your device (for example, using UserDefaults):

  • Study progress, confidence ratings, and spaced-repetition scheduling
  • Bookmarks, flags, and missed-question history
  • Practice and mock-exam history
  • Appearance and study-preference settings

This information stays on your device unless you back up or transfer the device through Apple system features outside this app’s control.

Notifications / reminders

CourtReady Lite may offer a local study reminder preference in Settings. The current release stores that preference on the device but does not schedule push or local notification deliveries.

Resetting local progress

You can reset locally stored study progress using Reset all progress in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes the app’s local data from the device, subject to normal device backup and restore behavior.

Children

The app is a general education study aid rated for a broad audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
