CourtReady Lite Support

CourtReady Lite is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of the New York State Unified Court System or any government agency.

CourtReady Lite helps you prepare for the New York State Court Officer exam with offline flashcards, Smart Review, practice sessions, mock exams, and on-device progress tracking for iPhone and iPad.

Basic troubleshooting

  • Force-quit the app and reopen it.
  • Confirm you are on the latest App Store update for CourtReady Lite.
  • Restart your device if a screen fails to load or progress looks stuck.
  • If content does not appear, reinstall the app after noting that local progress will be removed unless restored from a device backup.

Reset progress

Open Settings in CourtReady Lite and choose Reset all progress. This clears on-device study progress, bookmarks, and exam history stored by the app.
